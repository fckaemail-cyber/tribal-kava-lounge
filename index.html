Never Had Kava or Kratom? Start Here.

                        
Most people walk in with questions. This guide explains what we serve, how to choose your first drink, and how to enjoy responsibly.

                    


                    

                        

                            
Welcome

                            
You are not behind.

                            
Kava and kratom are new to a lot of people. We built the menu to make the experience easier with familiar flavors, clear labels, beginner-friendly options, and a team that is happy to help.

                        

                        

                            
Not Alcohol

                            
Is this alcohol?

                            
No. We are not a liquor bar. Our drinks are non-alcoholic, but kava and kratom products should still be treated as responsible-use botanical beverages.

                        

                    


                    

                        

                            
Deep Dive

                            
What is kava?

                            
Kava is a traditional plant-based beverage made from the root of the kava plant (Piper methysticum). Traditional kava has an earthy taste. Our Kava Clouds are designed to make the experience smoother and more familiar with creamy dessert-style flavors.

                            

                                ⚠️ Safety Note: Kava is not for everyone. Do not mix kava with alcohol or other substances. Talk to a qualified professional before using kava if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or have health concerns.
                            

                        

                        

                            
Deep Dive • 21+ Required

                            
What is kratom?

                            
Kratom is a botanical beverage made from kratom leaves (Mitragyna speciosa). We serve it as traditional-style brewed tea, fruit-forward refreshers, compact shots, and adult-use extracts. All kratom products are 21+ with valid ID — ask our team before ordering extracts.

                            

                                ⚠️ Safety Note: Kratom is intended for responsible adult use only. Do not use if pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or if you have health concerns without speaking with a qualified professional. Do not mix with alcohol or other substances.
                            

                            
Kratom products are 21+ only. Valid ID required.

Kava vs. Kratom: What’s the Difference?

                        
Both are botanical beverages, but they come from different plants, traditions, and serving styles. Here is a simple first-timer breakdown.

                    


                    
                    

                        

                            
                                

                                    Feature
                                    Kava
                                    Kratom
                                

                            
                            
                                

                                    Plant Source
                                    Root of the Piper methysticum shrub.
                                    Leaves of the Mitragyna speciosa tree.
                                

                                

                                    Traditional Origin
                                    Pacific Islands (Fiji, Tonga, Vanuatu) as a social welcome ritual.
                                    Southeast Asia (Thailand, Indonesia) as a traditional botanical tea.
                                

                                

                                    How We Serve It
                                    Crafted in dessert-style, creamy Kava Clouds.
                                    Served in fruit-forward, iced Kratom Refreshers.
                                

                                

                                    Flavor Profile
                                    Naturally earthy and peppery. Masked by creamy textures.
                                    Bitter herbal tea profile. Blended with fruit and citrus juice.
                                

                                

                                    Age Policy
                                    Available to responsible adults.
                                    Strictly 21+ only. Valid ID required.
                                

                                

                                    First-Time Recommendation
                                    Banana Pudding Kava Cloud
                                    Passionfruit Mint Kratom Refresher (21+)
                                

                                

                                    Responsible Use
                                    Do not mix with alcohol. Consult a professional if taking medications.
                                    For adults 21+ only. Do not mix with alcohol. Limit consumption.

Kava & Kratom FAQ

                        
Got questions? We have answers. Check out our beginner-focused guide to botanical drinks below.

What Is Kava?

                        
A clear, non-medical intro to kava drinks — culture, preparation, flavor, and how we serve them at Tribal Kava Lounge.

                    


                    

                        
The short version

                        
Kava is a traditional plant-based beverage made from the root of the kava plant (Piper methysticum), with deep roots in Pacific Island social culture. It is not alcohol. At Tribal Kava Lounge in West Palm Beach, we serve kava as traditional-style shells and as creamy dessert-style Kava Clouds designed for first-timers.


                        
Where kava comes from

                        
Kava has a long history of communal drinking in places like Fiji, Vanuatu, Tonga, and other Pacific communities. The plant is grown for its root, which is traditionally prepared into a shared drink for gatherings — not as a cocktail bar ingredient, but as a social ritual.


                        
How kava is prepared

                        
Traditionally, kava root is ground and mixed with water, then strained. The result is an earthy, bitter botanical beverage. Modern lounges often keep the root tradition while offering flavored formats so new guests are not forced to start with the hardest sip first.


                        
What it tastes like

                        
Raw traditional kava is often described as earthy, peppery, or “dirt smoothie” energy. That is normal for the plant — and exactly why we built Kava Clouds (Banana Pudding, Tres Leches, Blue Razz Coconut, Dubai Chocolate Pistachio, and more). Flavor first. No mystery menu energy.


                        
Kava at Tribal Kava Lounge

                        
    
                            
  • Kava shells — traditional-style format. $2 Tuesdays: single shells $2 from 2–5 PM.
    • 
                            
  • Kava Clouds — creamy, dessert-style drinks (typically $10).
    • 
                            
  • Non-alcoholic lounge vibe with free Wi‑Fi, free parking, pool tables, and multiple seating sections.
    • 
                        


                        
What kava is not

                        
Kava is not liquor. We do not market it as medicine, a treatment, or a guaranteed-effect product. People report different experiences, and experiences can vary. We focus on culture, flavor, hospitality, and responsible adult use.


                        
Responsible use

                        
Do not mix kava with alcohol or other substances. If you are pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or have health concerns, speak with a qualified professional before using kava. Products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
