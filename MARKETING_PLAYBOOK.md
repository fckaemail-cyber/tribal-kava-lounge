# Local customer-acquisition playbook

## Campaign links

Use one tracked link per channel so GA4 can attribute actual actions.

| Channel | URL |

| Google Business Profile | `https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=google_business_profile` |

## First 30 days

1. Keep the Google Business Profile complete and publish the weekly event lineup every Monday.
2. Put the tracked Instagram link in the bio and use the event link in story stickers.
3. Print one QR code for the tracked `/visit` link and place it on the counter, entrance, and partner flyers.
4. Ask nearby gyms, cafés, campuses, coworking spaces, and apartment communities to share the partner link.
5. Promote one simple reason to visit each day: $2 Tuesday, Wednesday karaoke, Thursday pool, Friday Lotería, and weekend late hours.
6. Share one Daily Kava local guide each week and point it to a real action: directions, event night, menu, or first visit.

## Connect web activity to in-store visits

Use four cashier/source codes: `GOOGLE`, `INSTAGRAM`, `FLYER`, and `PARTNER`. Ask “How did you hear about us?” on a first visit and tally the answer. Compare those counts with GA4 calls/directions and Business Profile direction requests every week.

Pause channels that produce clicks without calls, directions, inquiries, or visits. Repeat the creative and offer attached to the channels that produce real traffic.
